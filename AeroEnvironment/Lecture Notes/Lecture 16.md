## Bow Shocks
- occurs where solar wind goes super sonic to sub sonic
- 3-4 Earth radii in front of magneto sphere
- ranges in thickness from a few to 100s of km
- solar wind is slowed, heated, compressed (like a shock)
- between bow shock and magnetopause, **magnetosheath**, contains this ocmpressed heated solar wind plasma
- different from typical shocks vs aircraft
	- colisionless
	- stationary

## Magnetopause
- this occurs where solar wind dynamic pressure equals the magnetic pressure from magnetosphere
	- location actually varies
- solar wind essentially stops, forced to flow arund magnetosphere
	- why?
- some solar wind plasma enters the magnetosphere and become trapped to become plasma sheet
- **standoff distance** justification for the following equaiton

## Ring current
- source: energetic plasma from the tail moving inward with magnetic field (E x B drift)
- confined to 3-6 $R_E$
- caused by combination of the **ExB** drift (charge-independent), then gradient and curvature drifts (energy dependent) protons drift westard electrons drift eastwards
- average energy of 80 keV, most from 10-250 keV
	- current leads to reduction in the magnetic field in the inner magnetosphere, enhancement in the outer magnetosphere
- typically $10^-8~\mathrm{\frac{A}{m^2}};I \approx 1-3~\mathrm{MA}$
