    \caption{PRN5 C1C Pseudorange vs Expected Range Residuals}
    \label{fig:PRN5 C1C Pseudorange vs Expected Range Residuals}
\end{figure}

\hspace*{2em}We are able to observe the differences between the reported pseudoranges and
the expected range calculated previously in Figure \ref{fig:PRN5 C1C Pseudorange vs Expected Range}
and  Figure \ref{fig:PRN5 C1C Pseudorange vs Expected Range Residuals}. They generally match up, however there 
are residuals of up to $85\,\mathrm{km}$. The biggest differences between them are likely a result of the correction being 
done to find the expected range. The ephemeris broadcast, conversion to position, and subsequent correction we performed would
be much more accurate than the pseudorange that is being reported. The pseudorange afterall is affected by propogation effects in the
atmosphere (tropospheric effects, ionospheric effects) which cause these errors observed between the two ranging measurements 
being reported.

\hspace*{2em}Proof can be shown when examining these results for PRN 11 which is done for problem 5
as well. Examining Figures \ref{fig:PRN11 C1C Pseudorange vs Expected Range} and \ref{fig:PRN11 C1C Pseudorange vs Expected Range Residuals},
we see residuals in the same magnitude as seen in Figures \ref{fig:PRN5 C1C Pseudorange vs Expected Range} and \ref{fig:PRN5 C1C Pseudorange vs Expected Range Residuals}.
This means that throughout this process, the difference is not a result from using a different GPS satellite, PRN 11 and PRN 5
are in different satellite blocks and would be in view of NIST at different times through their orbits. The factors that would affect 
pseudoranging in this case that would not be due to the GPS satellites would be atmospheric effects and the clock errors in the GPS
receiver at the ground station. Additional evidence at this being a clock error issue is examining the clock
errors plotted from Problem 2, Figure \ref{fig:Clock Bias}. Here we can see an clock bias error of about $70\,\mathrm{km}$.
The same magnitude of error that we observe in the psuedorange and expected range residuals.
