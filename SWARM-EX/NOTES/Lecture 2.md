what is a cubesat
- there are "classes" of sats
- between ~1kg to ~10kg (or 20ish according to rick)
- came about from a problem of excess capacity
	- rockets desigend to carry more than primary payload
	- different insitutitons try to use this extra capacity
	- "secondary payloads"
- cubesat standard
	- there are also unit definitions
	- 1-3U cubesat
	- 6U
	- 12U
	- U = 10x10x11 cm cube
- why cubesats
	- low cost
		- highly standardized
	- excess capacity with rideshare, almost always LEO
		- good for earth sci/space weather
	- common platform for industry
