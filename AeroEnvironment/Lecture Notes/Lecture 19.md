## Galactic cosmic rays
- cosmic rays are not rays like gamma rays, they are particles*
	- 90% high energy protons
	- 9% $He^{++}$ alpha particles
	- 1% heavier nuclei
		- \*also some gamma rays
- originate primarily from within our galaxy
- radiation at earth's surface are created by *cosmic ray showers*

- never reach surface of earth directly
	- bc earth's atmosphere is very thick, like 10meters of ice with no collisions, hard as hell
- low energy higher fluxes are deflected by magnetosphere, but can penetrate in polar regions like SEPs
- peak of energy deposition is as 60,000 ft altitude (Pfotzer maximum)
- aviation industry crew are exposed to 2x higher radiation on average vs surface of earth
