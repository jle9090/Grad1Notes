## Storm Effects
- what happens to space envduring geo mag storm?
	- magnetosphere gets compressed, magnetopause moves inward
	- reconections and field line convections
	- solar wind /cme plasma injected into magnetopause
		- less "injected" more "get into to" - xinlin li
	- ring current gets enhanced 10x or more
		- ring current enhavmeentleads to reductin of B field on surface by 1-2% in hours, recovers in 1-2days
	- plasmasphere shrinks from erosion, plasma pause moves inward
	- rad belts get enhanced, 10-100x more high energy electrons and protons
	- aurorra occur; larger storms, aurroas reach lower latitudes
